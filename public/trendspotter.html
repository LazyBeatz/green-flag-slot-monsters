❓ How this machine works

The one big idea

Stocks do one of three things. They go up. They go sideways. They go down. This machine tells you which one — and the exact day it started.

Why day one matters

If you want to own a stock, the best time is day one of an up trend. And the day to let go is the first day it turns down. Slow to enter. Quick to exit.

The five wheels

📈 Trend: is the price above its 20-day average line?
🚀 Momentum: is it speeding up? (a tool called MACD)
💪 Strength: is the push strong? (a tool called DMI)
⛽ Fuel: is there gas left in the tank? (a tool called PPO)
🌊 Sector: is this stock's whole neighborhood winning right now?

The score

Every green wheel is worth 200 points. All five green = a perfect 1000. If sector data is resting, the score is out of 800 — that's the machine being honest, not broken.

The verdicts

⭐ PERFECT UP — everything is green and pulling together.
📈 TRENDING UP — above the line, speeding up, line rising.
🌱 RECOVERING — climbing back, but the line still points down. Not an up trend yet.
➡️ SIDEWAYS — mixed signals. The honest middle.
📉 TRENDING DOWN — below the line and slowing.
💀 FULL BEAR — down with the fuel running out too.

The sell day

The first day a stock closes below its 20-day line, the trend is done. No waiting, no wishing. Risk management doesn't ask permission.

The Money Truck

A perfect 1000 on a five-star stock in a winning sector brings out the truck. 🚚 For kicks, it can show a real option idea — the numbers are modeled, for learning only.

The fine print

Every spin uses historical signals from the anchor date on the machine. Nothing here is live. Nothing here is investment advice. It's a classroom dressed as a casino.
